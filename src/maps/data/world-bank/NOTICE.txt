World Bank Official Boundaries

The files in this folder (coarse.json, medium.json, fine.json) are simplified from World Bank Official
Boundaries (Admin 0 and Non-Determined Legal Status Areas), by the World Bank Group, licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source: https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0038272/world-bank-official-boundaries
Changes: territories sharing an ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code were merged; geometry was simplified to three
levels of detail and converted to TopoJSON with build.sh (mapshaper).

The boundaries, colors, denominations, and any other information shown on maps made with these files do
not imply any judgment on the part of The World Bank concerning the legal status of any territory or the
endorsement or acceptance of such boundaries.
